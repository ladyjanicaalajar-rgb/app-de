# Moana

> Walt Disney Animation Studios • 2016

## About
**Moana** is a 2016 American 3D computer-animated musical fantasy adventure film produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios and released by Walt Disney Pictures. Directed by Ron Clements and John Musker, it tells the story of a brave teenager who sets sail on a mission to save her people.

## Synopsis
Moana, the daughter of a Polynesian village chief, is chosen by the ocean to reunite a mystical relic with a goddess. She sets sail to find the legendary demigod Maui, hoping to return the heart of Te Fiti and heal her dying island. Together, they face monsters and storms — and Moana discovers her own identity and heritage.
